## Evidence meanings

- **Implemented**: the integration source/delivery contains the path.
- **Tested**: automated checks exercise the stated contract or resource.
- **Browser-inspected**: the live console was operated and viewed for the named
  scenario. It is not a pixel or timing comparison.
- **Native-compared**: Azahar's own native-resolution LCD capture was paired
  with the raw integrated production-browser LCD target at the same state,
  inputs and frame. A source fixture or source render is a separate bounded
  source tier and does not establish native/browser fidelity.
- **Incomplete**: a known adaptation, missing source, unsupported format or
  unverified route remains.

Historical cells below sometimes use “native-compared” for source fixtures or
source renders. Read their stated evidence literally; those cells do **not**
meet the raw Azahar-versus-production-browser definition above. The 25
September acceptance note at the end of this record governs future claims.
