Project modifications, 9 September 2026:
- Separated 68 rigid components while preserving the original 9,353 triangles.
- Removed the presentation transform, uniformly normalized width to 156 mm,
  fitted an X-axis 0–155-degree hinge and added independent control pivots.
- Corrected Blender's UV convention and restored original normal/tangent frames
  after export, preserving the source layout and tangent handedness.
- Added provisional live-display anchors and flagged two baked boot-artwork
  components for runtime replacement; their source geometry remains preserved.
- Replaced only red paint contributions in the body base-colour atlas with a
  silver appearance, preserving neutral ink and wear. The other six source
  image payloads and original material factors remain unchanged.
- Added a paint-region mask for restrained VGPU roughness variation at runtime;
  unpainted regions retain the source roughness treatment.

Additional modifications, 10 September 2026:
- Refined the broad lid and underside faces and added shallow curvature informed
  by Wesk's physical original 3DS XL/LL scan. The whole model now has 59,986
  triangles. Scan geometry is reference-only and is not included in this asset.
  Reference: https://bitbuilt.net/forums/threads/3ds-xl-ll-scan.7046/
- Preserved all seven silver-baseline texture payloads, UV alignment, material
  factors, control geometry and hierarchy. Updated shading frames to follow
  the smooth deformation.
- Subsequently added estimated fine silver paint grain to the normal and
  roughness atlases. Every unpainted pixel and the metallic channel remain
  exact; the other five embedded images and all geometry are unchanged.

- Subsequently replaced bounded underside regions with photographic EUR
  regulatory text, certification symbols and serial-label ink. The source
  wordmark is retained and repositioned. Reference images: the user-supplied
  image5 and the original silver-unit photograph from Konsolen-Chips:
  https://konsolen-chips.de/media/image/product/7758/lg/nintendo-3ds-xl-konsole-silber-schwarz-gebraucht~4.jpg
  Geometry, rig, other embedded images and atlas pixels outside the edit mask
  are unchanged. See docs/source-eur-validation.md for reconstruction limits.
  The photographic references are not represented as CC BY-licensed source
  material; the model author attribution above remains attached to this asset.

This public GLB is the fully textured silver model used by the portfolio
homepage, derived from model/candidates/joshua-xl/silver-eur.blend. It is not
the earlier textureless inspection preview. The original red source, its images
and the earlier procedural model remain preserved separately.

Known differences: the closed envelope is approximately 156 x 92.397 x 22.206 mm;
EUR artwork is photographic, with limited fine-detail resolution and
unverified barcode encoding; live displays use Nintendo's published active sizes but their placement
within the source glass remains provisional. The silver colour is an authored
adaptation, not a manufacturer measurement. Exact visual identity is not claimed.

Nintendo names and marks identify the depicted hardware. No endorsement by
Nintendo or by the original model author is implied.
